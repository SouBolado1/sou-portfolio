Better Life Calculator

An interactive web tool that turns MEXC’s 0% trading fee message into a personal dollar figure, and a shareable card each trader could post on X.

02

Objectives & strategic goals

Make MEXC’s 0% trading fee message personal: turn an abstract fee percentage into a yearly dollar figure and real things that money could buy.

Drive organic reach on X by giving traders and KOLs a personalised, shareable card to post, extending MEXC’s broader 0-fee story.

Pillar 01

Interactive web tool

Built with AP Collective: users enter their X handle and monthly trading volume to see what 0.4% fees cost them per year.

Pillar 02

Shareable savings card

Each result generates a branded card with the yearly fees recovered and what that money could buy instead.

Pillar 03

KOL QRT activation

KOLs quote-reposted MEXC’s campaign post with their own card and personal angle, seeding the format across X.

03

How it works

A simple four-step flow, from a trader’s own numbers to a post on X.

1

Enter your X handle

Personalises the card with the user’s own profile.

2

Pick a monthly volume

Type any amount or tap a preset: Casual $1K, Active $10K, Pro $100K, Whale $1M.

3

See your number

The yearly cost of 0.4% fees, e.g. $1M/mo = $48,000 a year.

4

Share the card on X

Post it or QRT the campaign with the card attached.

04

The savings card

Personalised

Carries the user’s own name and handle, so every post is unique content.

One big number

“A year of fees, recovered” makes the cost impossible to ignore.

Real-life trade-offs

Tokyo trips, therapy sessions, flights, hotel nights: fees turned into things people want.

Clear brand sign-off

0% Trading Fees · Trade More, Keep More.

05

Why it worked

Personal math beats generic promo

“0% fees” is easy to scroll past. “$48,000 a year” with your own name on it is not. The calculator made the value proposition specific to every trader.

Every card is its own post

Instead of one brand asset, the campaign produced a unique piece of content for each participant, giving KOLs and users a reason to share rather than just repost.

Lifestyle framing made fees tangible

Swapping percentages for trips, flights and hotel nights gave the message humour and relatability, which KOLs used to put their own spin on each QRT.
