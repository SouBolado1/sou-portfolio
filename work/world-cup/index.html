World Cup Prediction Card

A bracket builder launched as the knockouts began, turning every football fan’s picks into a shareable MEXC card and a points race.

02

Objectives & strategic goals

Put MEXC Prediction Markets in front of football fans at the peak of World Cup hype, as the group stage ended and the knockouts began.

Drive user-generated content on X by letting fans build and share their own bracket, with a reason to keep following as results came in.

Pillar 01

Bracket builder website

Fans enter their X handle and pick a winner for every knockout tie, from the Round of 32 to the champion.

Pillar 02

Shareable prediction card

Each bracket generates a personalised MEXC card, ready to post on X with #MEXCWorldCup.

Pillar 03

Points & rewards

Correct picks earn points. Top scorers win rewards on top of the campaign’s UGC rewards.

03

How it works

From the group stage to a shareable bracket in four steps.

1

Enter your X handle

Personalises the card with the fan’s own name and avatar.

2

Build your bracket

Pick winners across 16 knockout ties, all the way to the champion.

3

Get your card

A full MEXC-branded bracket with your champion pick front and centre.

4

Share & score

Post it on X, then earn points as your picks come true.

Built-in branding

05

Points & rewards

Two layers of incentive kept participants engaged beyond the first post.

01

Share your card

Posting the bracket on X qualified participants for the campaign’s UGC rewards.

02

Earn points

Every correct pick scored points as the knockout matches were played.

03

Climb the leaderboard

Participants with the most points earned extra rewards on top of the UGC rewards.

06

Why it worked

Riding the biggest moment

Launching as the Round of 32 was set tapped into a global conversation fans were already having, rather than trying to start a new one.

Brackets start debates

A champion pick is an opinion, and opinions get replies. Every card invited friends and followers to agree, argue or share their own.

A reason to come back

Points for correct picks gave participants a stake in every match, keeping MEXC Predict in the conversation through the knockout rounds.
